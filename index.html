    HOW TO PLAY

    CONTROLS (click a key to remap)

    Gamepad: D-pad/stick move · X light · Y heavy · A jump · B grab · RB block · LB dodge · RT run · LT eat · Back next item · Start start.

    FIGHTING

    • Tap LIGHT for combos. B.T. chains 4 hits, S.C. ends with a launching uppercut.
    • HEAVY after a light chain is a combo finisher.
    • GRAB an enemy in front of you. While holding: LIGHT = knee (3rd knocks down), HEAVY = throw. Hold a direction to throw that way. Thrown enemies bowl over others.
    • Hit enemies enough and they get dizzy — even bosses can be grabbed while dizzy or TIRED after a big special.
    • A flashing ! means a big attack is coming: dodge, jump or get out of the way.
    • Watch for the yellow lane warning: trams, taxis, bikes and golf carts hit everyone.

    FOOD & MONEY

    Enemies drop coins: whoever touches them first keeps them. Food is picked up with GRAB: eaten at once if it helps, otherwise stored in your bag. One wallet across the whole campaign: lira in Türkiye, dollars in the USA. Shops sell into your bag.
